# Mono Bold

Near-monochrome, with all the energy coming from oversized type. Black on white, pill-shaped buttons, one accent used once. It reads as athletic and confident — a brand that doesn't need decoration.

## Suits

Gyms and studios, sports clubs, coaches and trainers, barbers, streetwear and sneaker retail, anyone selling energy or transformation. Also works for a bold professional brand that wants presence without colour.

## Avoid for

Anything that needs to feel gentle, careful, or traditional — childcare, healthcare, heritage trades, bereavement services. Also avoid it when the client's brand palette is the point: this direction deliberately suppresses colour, so a client who loves their four brand colours will feel ignored.

## Posture

- **Type is the whole design.** Oversized headings with hard negative tracking, at a size that feels slightly too big — then keep it.
- **Colour is nearly absent.** Foreground and background carry the page; the accent appears once, on the primary action.
- **Rounded interactive shapes on a square layout.** Pill buttons, generous radius on images, but the grid stays straight.
- **Short, imperative copy.** "Start Monday." "Book a session." Verbs, not descriptions.
- **Photography is high contrast** — motion, effort, faces mid-action. No serene lifestyle shots.

## Do

- Set the hero headline to three or four words and let it fill the width.
- Use uppercase sparingly, and always with the caps tracking token — heavy uppercase without tracking is the fastest way to look amateur here.
- Keep sections few and decisive. Five strong sections beat nine polite ones.

## Don't

- Don't add a second colour to "warm it up". If the client needs warmth, use Warm Craft instead.
- Don't shrink the display type to fit a long headline. Cut the headline.
- Don't pair this with hedging copy. Bold shapes and cautious words read as a mistake.

## Fonts

Archivo at 400/600 for body and 700/800 for display, when the client has none. One family across the whole page, with weight doing the work.
