## What we collect

When you send us a message through this website, we receive what you enter in the form: your name, your email address, and your message.

## How we use it

We use it to answer you, and to keep a record of the conversation. We don't sell it, and we don't share it with anyone outside Riverside Community Arts.

## How long we keep it

We keep messages for two years, then delete them.

## Your choices

Write to us at [hello@example.org](mailto:hello@example.org) and we'll tell you what we hold about you, correct it, or delete it.
