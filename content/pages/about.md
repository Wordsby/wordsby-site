## Our story

Riverside Community Arts started in 1998 in a borrowed garage, with one pottery wheel and a handful of neighbors who wanted a place to make things together.

Today we're a nonprofit community arts center on the Springfield riverfront, with three studios, a gallery, and more than 1,200 students a year.

## What we believe

- **Art is for everyone.** Sliding-scale pricing means no one is turned away for lack of funds.
- **Beginners belong.** Most of our students had never taken an art class before Riverside.
- **Local artists make a community stronger.** Our gallery shows only artists from the region.

## The gallery

Our gallery hosts a new exhibition every month, featuring local artists and our students' work. Openings are on the first Friday of the month, and they're always free.
